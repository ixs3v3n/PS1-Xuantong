COMPUTATIONAL ECONOMICS GAME

Online Marketplace Reputation

You are a buyer shopping on an online marketplace. In each round, you will see a different product and a seller's reputation information.

How to Play

  • You receive a new $100 budget in every round.
  • You will evaluate the same seller under three information conditions.
  • You will choose a maximum bid for No History, Recent History, and Full History.
  • The seller's asking price is hidden until the results are revealed.
  • You must submit all three bids before seeing any transaction results.
  • The seller and asking price are the same across all three conditions within a round.

How is surplus calculated?

If a trade occurs, buyer surplus = True Value − Your Bid. If your bid is below the seller's asking price, no trade occurs.

Research Question

How much reputation information is enough to support successful cooperation between unfamiliar users on an online marketplace?

This game is a small-scale demonstration of the proposed research design. Results from individual players are not sufficient to establish general empirical conclusions.
